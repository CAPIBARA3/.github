# Hi there 👋, we are CAPIBARA!

### CAPIBARA stands for...
**C**ollaboration for the **A**nalysis of **P**hotonic and **I**onic **B**ursts and **R**Adiation

### Our aim is...
to explore the high energy cosmos by planning, developing, launching and operating two missions. The first consisting of an hosted instrument for cosmic ray detection in Earth's orbit. The second is a CubeSat constellation for the precise localization of gamma-ray and X-ray astrophysical phenomena.

### Our team is...
student-led, passionate and motivated. Meet each of [our members](https://capibara3.github.io/team).

Our [website](https://capibara3.github.io) to find more information and to contact us [here](https://capibara3.github.io/contact). Join us on this exciting journey—we are thrilled to have you onboard! 🚀
